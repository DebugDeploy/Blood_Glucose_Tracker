## Why Dark Theme?

1. **Reduces Eye Strain:** Especially in low-light conditions
2. **Modern Look:** Aligns with current design trends
3. **Better Focus:** Draws attention to important data
4. **Energy Efficient:** OLED screens use less power with dark pixels
5. **Professional:** Popular for data dashboards and health apps
